This policy explains how Lawpilotz handles information when you use our website, demo, or legal-document analysis service.

Information we collect

We may collect account and contact information, messages you send us, document content submitted for analysis, and technical usage information such as browser and device data.

How we use information

We use information to operate and improve Lawpilotz, process requested document analysis, communicate with you, provide support, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Documents and sensitive information

Only submit documents you are authorized to share. Avoid providing personal data or confidential information unless your organization has assessed the service for that use. Do not use the demo to submit sensitive documents.

Service providers and sharing

We may use carefully selected service providers to host the service, process data, deliver communications, and support operations. They may handle information only to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect people, rights, and systems.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Some retention may be necessary for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@lawpilotz.space.
